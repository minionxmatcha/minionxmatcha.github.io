### The Fresh Micro-Mill Experience

* **Fresh-mill upgrade: Okumidori single origin (first flush, 2026 harvest, 一番茶):** +$1 — Add to any drink. Ground directly on the stone mill counter in small micro-batches to preserve aroma and prevent oxidation.

### Matcha Lattes (3 Flavour Profiles)

* **House Blend (特製合組):** $8.50 — Balanced. Moderate umami, moderate bitterness, light–medium body.
* **Okumidori (おくみどり):** $12.50 — Savoury and rich. High umami, low bitterness, heavy velvety body.
* **Soemon Blend (想右衛門):** $13.50 — Silky and deep. Low umami, clean bitterness, robust body.
