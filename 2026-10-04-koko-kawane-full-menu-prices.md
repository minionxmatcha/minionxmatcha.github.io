### The Fresh Micro-Mill Experience
* **Okumidori Single Origin (First Flush – 2026 Harvest, Ichibancha / 一番茶):** Available as a +$1 fresh-mill upgrade for your beverage. Ground directly on the stone mill counter in small micro-batches to preserve aroma and prevent oxidation.

---

### Matcha Lattes (3 Flavor Profiles)

* **House Blend (特製合組):** $8.50  
  * *Profile: Balanced* — Moderate umami, moderate bitterness, balanced light-medium body.
* **Okumidori (おくみどり):** $12.50  
  * *Profile: Savoury & Rich* — High umami, low bitterness, heavy velvety body.
* **Soemon Blend (想右衛門):** $13.50  
  * *Profile: Silky & Deep* — Low umami, pleasant clean bitterness, robust body.

---

### Pure Usucha (Thin Tea)

* **Single Origin Cups ($7.00 each — Hot or Iced):**
  * **Tsuyuhikari (つゆひかり):** Gentle and floral.
  * **Okumidori (おくみどり):** Bold, savoury, and punchy.
* **Usucha Flight ($17.00 — Hot Only):**
  * Includes 3 side-by-side comparative bowls: **Tsuyuhikari**, **Soemon**, and **Okumidori**.  
  *(Note: Soemon is exclusive to the flight and latte—it is not sold as a standalone single cup).*

---

#### Matcha Cakes:
* **Midoba (翠葉):** $10.00 — Matcha with Raspberry core.
* **Akane (茜空):** $12.00 — Matcha with Sweet Mango insert.
* **Hisui (翡翠):** $14.00 — Geometric multifaceted green gem filled with Matcha and Strawberry.

#### Hojicha Cakes:
* **Kohaku (琥珀):** $10.00 — Roasted Hojicha paired with Passionfruit.
* **Kirizakura (霧桜):** $12.00 — Roasted Hojicha paired with Peach.

#### Ochagashi (Cake & Tea Pairing Sets — $15.00 each)
Save money compared to ordering à la carte:
* **Gentle Pairing:** Tsuyuhikari Usucha + Akane (Matcha & Mango) = $15.00
* **Bold Pairing:** Okumidori Usucha + Kohaku (Hojicha & Passionfruit) = $15.00

---

### Hojicha (3 Preparation Styles)

* **Hojicha Latte ($8.00):** Medium roast combining both stems and leaves for a creamy, well-rounded cup.
* **Karigane Hojicha ($7.00):** Roasted stem tea offering lighter body with natural sweetness.
* **Kasuga Hojicha ($7.00):** Roasted leaf tea offering a richer, deeper, toastier aroma.
