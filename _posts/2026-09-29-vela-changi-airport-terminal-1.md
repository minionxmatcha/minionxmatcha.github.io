### The Matcha: Cultivars & Grades

Both matcha tiers feature single-cultivar **Yabukita** sourced directly from **Mie Prefecture, Japan**:

* **Sundown Matcha (Specialty Grade):** Tasting notes of roasted chestnut, leafy greens, and wheat. ($25 for 30g tin)
* **Sunrise Matcha (Epicurean Grade):** Tasting notes of seaweed umami, honey sweetness, and light florals. ($36 for 30g tin / +$1 upgrade for drinks)

---

### Drink Menu & Pricing

#### Classics
* **Matcha Latte:** $9.00
* **Clear Matcha:** $8.50

#### Signatures & Specials
* **Ube Vanilla Matcha:** $9.50
* **Seasonal Off-Menu Special ("Autumn in Bangkok"):** In collaboration with Kaew Boutique. Features maple & roasted chestnut infused Sundown matcha cream layered over sweet coconut water with young coconut chunks, topped with crispy thongmuan feuilletine and pandan dust.

#### Customisation Options
* **Alternative Milks:** +$1.00 (Oat, Soy, Almond)
* **Syrups & Flavours:** +$0.50
* **Extra Shots:** Espresso shot (+$1 / +$1.50), Extra matcha shot (+$1 / +$1.50)
* **Toppings & Cold Foam:** +$1.00

*(Note: Check your takeaway cup lid before you sip—every lid features a cheeky quote!)*
